# Frequently asked questions

## What does the application do?

It researches public competitor and target-account relationships, uses configured providers and Gemini for analysis, and presents findings with confidence information and CSV export.

## Are API keys safe in the browser?

No. Keep them in server-side environment variables and never expose them in client bundles.

## Is the output authoritative?

No. Results are research assistance and may be incomplete, stale, or incorrect. Verify important findings against primary sources.

## Does the application use a database?

Not currently. Results are processed during requests and exported by the application.
