# Security Policy

## Secrets

Keep `GEMINI_API_KEY` and `SERP_API_KEY` in local environment configuration. Never expose them in client bundles, logs, screenshots, commits, or public issues. Rotate credentials immediately if exposed.

## Reporting

Report vulnerabilities privately to Brajesh Kumar (`brajesh31`) with the affected route or file, reproduction steps, impact, and mitigation. Do not include real customer or account data.

## Safe data collection

Respect source terms, robots policies, rate limits, privacy requirements, and applicable law when collecting public web data.
